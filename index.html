About us

Everything a good stay needs, and nothing that gets in the way

Fortune Vista Stay Inn is a newly opened property in Hafeezpet, built for people who want a room that is genuinely clean, a bed that is genuinely comfortable, and a front desk that answers. No queues, no fuss.
